**Organization:** DigitalMind  
**Role:** Infrastructure Architect  
**Period:** February 2021 – February 2022

## Context

Redesign the production infrastructure to take advantage of containerization and Kubernetes orchestration for the projects hosted by DigitalMind.

## My contribution

- Researched Kubernetes capabilities and evaluated cloud offerings from AWS, Google Cloud and DigitalOcean.
- Defined project infrastructure through YAML manifests and developed Helm charts.
- Designed the architecture for shared services, including MySQL, NFS and Elasticsearch.
- Worked on observability using Loki, Prometheus and Grafana.
- Addressed automatic scaling for periods of peak demand.

## Engineering focus

Connecting application deployment, shared infrastructure and operational visibility within a container-based production platform.

This work was carried out at DigitalMind as part of its production infrastructure modernization.
