**Type:** Personal project  
**Status:** Work in progress

## Context

Make vehicle running-cost assumptions visible and adjustable, so users can explore how mileage, energy prices and charging habits affect an indicative annual comparison.

## Implementation

- Comparison across vehicle categories and fuel types.
- Configurable annual mileage, usage period and share of home charging.
- Cost breakdowns covering energy, estimated maintenance, vehicle tax and insurance.
- Editable fuel and electricity prices, with references to MIMIT and ARERA sources.
- Installable web application with a web manifest and service worker.

## Engineering focus

Turning a practical everyday question into an interactive tool with explicit assumptions, rather than presenting a single unexplained estimate.

The figures are indicative running-cost estimates, not quotes or a complete vehicle purchase-cost analysis. Actual costs depend on the vehicle and individual circumstances.

[Browse the published application files on GitHub](https://github.com/setola/costo-auto-italia). The public repository currently contains the distribution files, rather than a separate development source tree.
